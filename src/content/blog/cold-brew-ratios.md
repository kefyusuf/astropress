Cold brewing is a game of patience. The stronger the ratio, the fuller the body.

## 1:8 — Intense and concentrated

One part coffee to eight parts water. Produces an ideal concentrate for diluting over ice.

## 1:12 — Balanced and ready to drink

One part coffee to twelve parts water. Drinkable straight, no dilution needed.

> Rule: keep the grind coarse, steep 12–16 hours, in the fridge.
