The V60 puts you in control. Three variables: grind, water temperature, pour speed.

1. **Bloom:** wet with 2:1 water, wait 30 seconds.
2. **Pour:** slow spirals from the center outward.
3. **Time:** aim for 3 minutes total.

Water temperature between 92–96°C; hotter for light roasts.
